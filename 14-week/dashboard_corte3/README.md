**Dataset:** Simulated beverage sales, 2025

## Dashboard & insight

This interactive Power BI dashboard analyzes simulated beverage sales during 2025 across three locations in Huila, Colombia: Neiva, Garzón, and Pitalito.

The dashboard includes three main visualizations: monthly sales trends, sales by product, and sales by location. These charts help identify sales patterns and compare commercial performance.

Two slicers allow users to filter the information by location and quarter. A date hierarchy supports drill-down analysis, while cross-filtering allows users to explore relationships between the visualizations.

The main insight is that Coca-Cola Zero generates the highest total sales revenue, while Neiva has the strongest sales performance among the three locations. December also records the highest monthly revenue.

Based on these findings, the recommendation is to strengthen promotional strategies for Sprite and Fanta and improve sales performance in Pitalito.

The dashboard highlights key indicators, including December sales, Coca-Cola Zero sales, and Neiva sales.

All data used in this project is simulated for academic purposes and does not represent actual Coca-Cola sales.

## Dashboard features

- Three comparative visualizations.
- Two interactive slicers: location and quarter.
- Date hierarchy with drill-down.
- Cross-filtering between charts.
- Highlighted sales indicators.
- Context, findings, and business recommendations.

# Model & questions

The star schema was designed to analyze the sales process of a technology store.
The main fact table is called Fact_Ventas, and it stores the information related to each sale.
Its main measures are quantity, unit price, discount, and total sales amount.
The fact table is connected to the Time, Product, Customer, and Store dimensions.
These dimensions allow the company to analyze sales from different points of view.
The first business question is: Which products generate the highest sales revenue each month?
The second business question is: Which store generates the highest sales amount each month and contributes the most to the monthly sales target?
This model can help the company understand its sales performance and make better business decisions.
